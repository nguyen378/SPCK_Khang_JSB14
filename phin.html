Vietnamese Phin Coffee

Strong. Bold. Traditional.

What is Phin Coffee?

Vietnamese Phin Coffee is a traditional coffee brewed using a small metal filter called a Phin.

Flavor

It has a rich, strong, and bold flavor. Vietnamese Phin Coffee is often served with sweetened condensed milk for a sweet and creamy taste.

How It Is Made

Ground coffee is placed inside a metal Phin filter. Hot water is then added, allowing the coffee to slowly drip into a cup.
