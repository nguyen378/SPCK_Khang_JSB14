Latte

Smooth. Creamy. Comforting.

What is Latte?

Latte is a popular coffee drink made with espresso and a large amount of steamed milk. It is known for its smooth, creamy texture and mild coffee flavor.

Flavor

Latte has a mild, creamy, and slightly sweet flavor. The steamed milk makes the coffee smoother and less bitter.

How It Is Made

A latte is made by combining a shot of espresso with steamed milk and a thin layer of milk foam on top.
