Cappuccino

Rich. Creamy. Balanced.

What is Cappuccino?

Cappuccino is a popular Italian coffee drink made with espresso, steamed milk, and a thick layer of milk foam.

Flavor

It has a rich coffee flavor combined with creamy milk. The taste is smooth, balanced, and slightly sweet.

How It Is Made

A cappuccino is traditionally made with equal parts espresso, steamed milk, and milk foam.
